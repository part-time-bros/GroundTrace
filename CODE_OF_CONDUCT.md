# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique the code, not the person.

## What that means here

- Technical disagreement is welcome and useful; personal attacks, harassment, and demeaning
  comments are not.
- "This is wrong, and here's why" is fine. "You're an idiot for writing this" is not.
- Nobody owes you free work. Maintainers and contributors participate voluntarily.

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else the project is represented.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a private security advisory (the only private
channel this repo has) or contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block accounts.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
kept short deliberately.
